The Offer Ladder

Four offers, each one complete on its own. The ladder exists for the members who want to climb it, never as a trapdoor under the ones who don't.

Starter Tier
Under $10 /month
Purpose: An easy first yes with fast wins.
  • ✔Start Here course
  • ✔Welcome walkthrough
  • ✔Community orientation
  • ✔5-question quiz
  • ✔Personalized learning path
  • ✔2 to 3 flagship courses
Examples: Infinite Visibility Challenge, Launch Challenge, AI Resource Library
Premium Tier
$27–33 /month
Purpose: Higher implementation support and retention.
  • ✔Premium resources
  • ✔Advanced templates
  • ✔Live coaching
  • ✔Exclusive trainings
  • ✔More implementation
Examples: Create Your Own Infinite Challenge, Sign Your Next High Ticket Client, Advanced AI Artifacts
Strategy Audit
$99 / 60 min
Purpose: Personal implementation support and clarity.
  • ✔Completed action plan
  • ✔Personalized strategy
  • ✔Clear roadmap
You ask one honest question: "Do you feel confident implementing this on your own, or would more support help?" No pressure. No pitching everyone. Only strong-fit clients get an invitation, and everyone else walks away with a complete plan they paid $99 for and would happily pay again.
High Ticket
$1k–15k
Purpose: Deep, customized implementation.
  • ✔More guidance
  • ✔More accountability
  • ✔More customization
  • ✔More speed
High ticket should never exist because you need money. It exists because certain people genuinely want more guidance, more accountability, more customization, and more speed. When that is true, a premium invitation is a gift, not a squeeze.

The High Ticket Philosophy

The mindset that makes this whole ecosystem ethical, sustainable, and genuinely enjoyable to run.

The
Flywheel
Recurring revenue creates stability. High ticket creates depth. Depth improves the community, community improves your content, better content improves your ads, and ads grow recurring revenue. Round and round the flywheel goes, and every turn makes the next one easier.
Every offer is complete. Members never need to upgrade. They only upgrade if they genuinely want deeper support. A funnel pushes people forward; an ecosystem lets them settle wherever they thrive.
Your organic content is better spent building authority than selling an $8/month product. Paid acquisition builds recurring revenue faster. Ads aren't mandatory for every business, but they become increasingly valuable when the goal is a large low-ticket membership, because the economics of an $8 offer often don't justify extensive manual selling.
They provide immediate value, build trust, and create clarity. And they naturally reveal, without any selling, whether additional support would genuinely help. The audit is the world's gentlest qualification process because the client wins either way.

Never sell everyone

✗ MythEveryone should buy coaching. More upgrades always means a healthier business.
✓ TruthOnly people who want it, need it, can implement it, and can afford it should be invited. Everyone else should feel completely at home right where they are.

Should I offer high ticket yet?

A 20-second gut check before you build a premium offer.

Have you achieved this result yourself?
Can you help someone get there individually?
✦ Start at $1,000.

You have the result and the ability to guide someone to it. Open a small number of spots, invite only strong fits from your audits, and raise the price as your proof stacks up.

Gain experience first.

That is not a no, it is a not-yet. Keep serving your membership, deliver Strategy Audits, and collect wins. High ticket built on real proof sells itself later.
